Website Evaluation

Website URL: Animalia.bio

Website Name: Animalia

Target Audience

This is an animal encyclipedia that provides information and fun facts about many kinds of animals. It's target audience would be animal enthusiasts or those who simply need information on a specific animal.

Site Organization

The website uses hierarchal organization with larger pages that lead to more specific ones as seen on the homepage and its subsections.

C.R.A.P. Principle used

The most obviously adhered to principle is proximity. Each section of the website is grouped by relation. (The different large categories of animals being circled around animalia as a whole for example.)

Accessibility Audit

The website failed the audit pretty badly, scoring only a 12. The main issues are with sight accessibility, some text is light grey on a white background, some elements prevent zooming, and links are not meaningfully marked in any way.

Site Effectiveness

Despite its accessibility issues the website does its job pretty well. There are many different sections for searching animals by all kinds of attributes as well as a search bar for if you need to find a specific animal.

Site Efficiency

As stated in the previous section the website has a search bar and its right at the top as soon as the page loads so anything you may need to find is only a click or two away.

Site Engagement

The website is pretty pleasant and fun to use as it uses nice graphics and offers fun sections like "Animal of the Day" and "intersting fact of the day".

My Reccomendation

I would suggest the site add more context between the headings and the background so they are easier to see as well as resize some of the sections to feel less messy as they feature different shapes and organization stylees per section.
